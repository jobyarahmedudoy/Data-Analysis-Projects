# HR Survey Analysis
This project is based on a guided analysis from Maven Analytics, focused on exploring and visualizing employee survey data from the Seattle Department of Public Works. The dataset contains responses from nearly 1,500 public works employees, covering job satisfaction, motivation, and engagement levels.

## Project Overview

As an HR Associate, your task is to analyze the survey dataset and prepare a clean, insight-driven visual summary for the HR leadership team.
The goal is to help the department understand overall employee sentiment and identify opportunities to improve workplace experience.
